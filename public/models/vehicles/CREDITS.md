# Vehicle models: credits and licences

## What is shipped here

All `*.glb` files in this folder (geometry and every embedded texture) are **original work made for
Spider-Man: Harare**. They were generated by the scripts in `tools/vehicles/` (Blender 5.0 Python + PIL),
so no third-party mesh, photo or texture is embedded. You can rebuild them with
`tools/vehicles/build_all.sh`.

- **Licence:** the shape data, textures and scripts are offered under **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication),
  unless the repository's own licence says otherwise.
- **Real vehicles:** the bodies copy the *proportions and published dimensions* of real models (Toyota HiAce
  H100, Honda Fit GE, Toyota Corolla Axio E160, Mercedes-Benz W204, Toyota Wish AE10, Toyota Hilux AN20/30,
  Toyota Land Cruiser 200, Isuzu N-series, a Yutong/FAW-class city bus). **No manufacturer logos, badges or
  wordmarks are reproduced**: grille emblems are plain chrome ellipses and rings. Model names are used only
  descriptively in the docs.
- **Liveries:** 'ZUPCO', 'ZIMBABWE UNITED PASSENGER COMPANY', 'POLICE' and 'ZRP' appear as plain text
  set in DejaVu Sans Bold, because the brief asks for the real Harare liveries. They are not the official
  logos, and their colours are approximate (see `docs/references/STREETLIFE.md`). The truck's
  'CHIPO & SONS WHOLESALE DISTRIBUTORS' and the fleet number 'ZB 1147' are invented. All number plates are
  invented.

## Existing free models that were checked and rejected

Poly Haven (CC0) has no road vehicles, only props such as `covered_car` and `old_tyre`. OpenGameArt's
CC0 / CC-BY vehicle packs (`free-low-poly-vehicles-pack`, `car-kit`, `psx-style-cars`, `pick-up-truck` and
others) are low-poly and stylised, and none of them is a HiAce, a Fit, a Wish or a ZUPCO bus. Kenney and
Quaternius are too low-poly. Sketchfab and CGTrader need a login and non-CC0 terms, so they were not used.
