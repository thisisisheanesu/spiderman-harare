## Environment map

`env/harare_day_ibl_1k.hdr`: [Poly Haven wide_street_01](https://polyhaven.com/a/wide_street_01) by Sergej Majboroda, CC0 1.0. Processing: sun disc clamped to relative luminance 12.0 (removed 75 % of the total energy); ground below -3 deg progressively blurred (sigma 18 px) so reflections show a soft ground tone, not the source street; sky and horizon unchanged.
